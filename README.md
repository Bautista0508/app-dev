# Sentenced to Be a Hero
My first repository
**My favorite Movie is Sentenced to Be a Hero**
Sentenced to Be a Hero is a dark‑fantasy series about criminals who are punished by being forced to serve as “Heroes” in a brutal war, following wrongly accused ex‑knight Xylo Forbartz as he leads a penal unit and forms a pact with the goddess Teoritta to survive and uncover the truth behind his sentence.

Core Premise:
- The “Hero” title is not an honor—it is the harshest criminal punishment.
- Those convicted of serious crimes are forced into military service, fighting on the front lines.
- If they die, they are revived and sent back into battle, making the punishment endless.
